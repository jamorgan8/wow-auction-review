AUCTION REVIEW - QUICK START
============================

A price-trend dashboard for the World of Warcraft auction house, built on the
data the Auctionator addon saves. Everything runs on your own PC.

YOU NEED
  - The Auctionator addon installed in WoW.
  - At least one auction house scan with Auctionator, then log out or type /reload
    (WoW only saves addon data when you log out or /reload).

START
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. your Desktop).
  2. Double-click AuctionReview.exe. Your browser opens the dashboard.
     - Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed.
       Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     - If it can't find WoW, it asks you to pick your World of Warcraft folder.
  3. Leave the black window open while you play. It picks up new data each time
     you /reload or log out. Close the window to stop it.

ITEM NAMES (recommended)
  The dashboard shows item IDs until it knows names. Click "Install addon" on the
  dashboard, or copy the AuctionReviewNames folder from this zip into
    World of Warcraft\<_retail_ / _classic_ / ...>\Interface\AddOns
  Then log in, wait about a minute, and type /reload.

YOUR DATA
  Price history is kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AuctionReview and keeps growing past
  Auctionator's 21-day limit. Delete that folder to start over.
